FAQ

    We are open daily from 7:00 AM to 12:00 AM. Check the opening hours table on our home page for details.

  2. Do you offer dairy-free milk?

    Yes! We offer oat and almond milk for all our drinks at no extra cost.

  5. How can I share my opinion about the café?

    We'd love to hear from you! Visit our Rate Us page and leave your review.
